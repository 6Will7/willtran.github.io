Import from Letterboxd

Bring a Letterboxd diary in under the name above. Films already logged here are left exactly as they are — imports only add what's missing.

Sync pulls your latest ~50 diary entries (public profiles only). For your full history: on Letterboxd go to Settings → Data → “Export your data”, unzip the download, and import the CSV files here — diary.csv, ratings.csv and watchlist.csv all work, and you can pick several at once.

Heads-up: this diary is star-based, so Letterboxd entries with no rating can't be imported.
