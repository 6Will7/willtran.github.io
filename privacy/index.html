Privacy & Terms

The short version: this is a personal site. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics trackers, and nothing you do here is sold or shared for marketing. The longer version is below. Last updated November 4, 2026.

What this site collects

  • Things you type into forms. The guestbook, suggestion box, and restaurant submission form ask for a name and your entry. Submissions are held for review and, if approved, published with the name you gave. The movies page similarly stores the display name, ratings, and reviews that friends enter in their diaries.
  • Basic technical data. Like most sites, the server sees your IP address. It is used to run rate limits and block abuse (for example, limiting how often a form or a photo like can be submitted) — not to profile you.
  • Settings stored in your own browser. Your theme and accent-color choices, BART favorites, gallery likes, and your stocks watchlist are kept in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device unless you use the optional watchlist sync, which stores just your ticker list on the server under a random code, with no name attached.

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and it does not sell personal information.

Other services involved

Some features pull data from outside services, and when your browser loads that content those services receive your IP address, as with any website: photos are hosted by Cloudinary, movie posters and data come from TMDB, train departures come from BART, and market data comes from sources including Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, the SEC, and FRED. The site itself runs on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare.

Public submissions

  • By submitting to the guestbook, suggestion box, or restaurant list, you agree that your name and entry may be published on this site after review.
  • Only submit things you have the right to share, and nothing unlawful or abusive. Submissions that don't meet that bar won't be published, or will be removed.
  • Want something of yours removed or changed? Send a note through the Suggestion Box (category: Something else) saying what to take down, and it will be removed — no questions, no hard feelings.

The content itself

  • Everything here is personal and informational. Market data may be delayed or occasionally wrong, and nothing on this site is financial advice.
  • Photos and writing on this site are © Will Tran unless noted otherwise. Movie posters belong to their respective owners and are shown via TMDB.
  • This page may change as the site does; the date at the top reflects the latest version.
