Changelog

What's new on willtran.org — features and changes, newest first.

November 2026

Nov 28

Terminal-style command bar — press backtick (`) anywhere on the site and type a plain-word command: aapl overview, msft holders, movers, screener, compare aapl msft nvda. Commands are deliberately plain English, not borrowed terminal mnemonics. New Movers page (gainers, losers, most active, world indices), Compare page (several tickers on one normalized return scale), and Screener (3,002 stocks by valuation, growth, yield — dataset building, refreshed quarterly). The Stocks page gains a one-screen overview (key ratios, returns, analyst snapshot), a dividends block (5-year history, yield, frequency) and a historical prices table with CSV download. Worker routes added for Yahoo-sourced movers, indices, dividends and price history — deploy the worker to switch them on.

Nov 17

Institutional holders (13F) — every stock page now shows its top institutional holders from SEC Form 13F filings: shares, market value, and the change vs last quarter, plus new positions and exits.

Nov 14

Gallery slideshow — full-screen, shuffled, auto-advancing. The jump menu (⌘K) now also searches BART stations and photo captions.

Nov 12

The Wire — company news, SEC filings and earnings for the tickers you follow, in one stream. Filings got topic labels (an 8-K now says "Leadership change" or "Earnings results" instead of just "8-K"). New Earnings This Week page: who reports, when, and what the Street expects. The BART page gained a San Francisco weather strip.

Nov 10

Letterboxd import for Movies — bring your diary, ratings and watchlist over in one pass. New What to Watch page (popular movies by streaming service) and a Stats tab for the group's watching habits. Rates page: the change column now reads in basis points. Stocks: SEC filings got a pager — browse the full history, not just the latest two dozen.

Nov 5–6

New accent presets (Royal, Frutiger) in the "Paint the site" switcher. The jump menu learned to search recipes, restaurants and logged movies. A warmer, more colorful design pass: cream background, tinted cards, color-blocked homepage.

October 2026

Oct 26

Admin portal — every management page (gallery, restaurants, guestbook, recipes, movies, suggestions) in one place, with pending-count badges.

Oct 25

Suggestion box opens — suggest a movie, restaurant, recipe or site feature. Movies admin for managing friends' reviews.

Oct 21

New navigation — a searchable jump menu (⌘K, or "Menu" up top) replaces the old dropdowns, and a Sitemap page lists everything in one place.

Oct 20

Correlation calculator — how closely do two stocks actually move together, across six timeframes?

Oct 17

BART tracker — real-time departures with line colors, a system map, service advisories, and a nearest-station finder covering BART, Muni and VTA.

Oct 14

Movies — friends' film diaries with star ratings, reviews and watchlists. Economic Data — the U.S. release calendar (CPI, payrolls, FOMC) with a countdown to the next big one. Recipe manager for keeping the cookbook tidy.

Oct 13

Guestbook with an approval queue, photo likes and share buttons across the site, and a gallery manager (recategorize, caption, reorder). A design refresh: Newsreader headlines, glassy nav, and the "Paint the site" accent switcher. Rates gains an as-of date picker.

Oct 10

Restaurants — a Bay Area list with cuisine filters and a public submission form (submissions land in an approval queue).

Oct 1–2

Stocks watchlist syncs across devices with pairing codes — no passwords. Insider trades and SEC financials arrive on the stock pages.

Older history predates this page. Want something built? The suggestion box is open.
